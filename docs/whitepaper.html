Calculation logic

Daily growth rate:

$$ g_t \;=\; \frac{c_t}{c_{t-1}} - 1 $$
POSITION
Signed share quantity $q$ from the open book.

Notional risk uses $|q|\,c_T$.

GR
$$ \mathrm{GR} = \bar g = \tfrac{1}{n}\sum g_t $$
DOWNSIDE GR
$$ \mathrm{Downside\,GR} = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{|D|}\sum_{t \in D} g_t & D = \{t : g_t < 0\} \neq \emptyset \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$
DAILY STDEV
$$ \sigma_g = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{n-1}\sum (g_t - \bar g)^2} $$
DOWNSIDE STDEV
$$ \sigma_g^{-} = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{n}\sum \bigl(\min(g_t,0)\bigr)^2} $$
LATEST DAY (Z)
$$ z_T = \begin{cases} \dfrac{g_T - \bar g}{\sigma_g} & \sigma_g > 0 \\ 0 & \sigma_g = 0 \end{cases} $$
1-STDEV MOVE
$$ |q| \cdot c_T \cdot \sigma_g $$
30D
$$ R_{W} = \frac{c_T}{c_{T-W}} - 1 $$
60D Z-PATH
$$ \bigl(z_t\bigr)_{t \in W}, \qquad z_t = \frac{g_t - \bar g_W}{\sigma_{g,W}} $$

Sequence of $z$-scores over the window, not a scalar. Empty in the table when the path is not exported for that row.
